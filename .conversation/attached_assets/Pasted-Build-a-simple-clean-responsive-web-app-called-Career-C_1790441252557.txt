Build a simple, clean, responsive web app called **Career Compass**.

### Purpose

Career Compass is an 8-question career-interest quiz. It should help users explore broad career directions based on their interests. It is NOT a career prediction or professional assessment.

### Career directions

Use these 8 directions:

1. Business & Entrepreneurship
2. Technology & Engineering
3. Science & Research
4. Creative & Design
5. People & Communication
6. Health & Wellbeing
7. Law, Government & Society
8. Environment & Sustainability

### Questions

**Question 1: Which project sounds most interesting?**

* Start a small business → Business
* Build an app → Technology
* Research how something works → Science
* Create a design or video → Creative

**Question 2: Which activity would you enjoy most?**

* Help someone improve their health → Health
* Debate an important issue → Law
* Build or fix something → Technology
* Teach or talk with people → People

**Question 3: What would you choose for a free afternoon?**

* Run an experiment → Science
* Plan a business idea → Business
* Create something → Creative
* Learn about nature or climate → Environment

**Question 4: Which problem would you like to solve?**

* Make technology better → Technology
* Improve people's health → Health
* Help a business grow → Business
* Improve society or government → Law

**Question 5: Which activity sounds most interesting?**

* Design a brand or website → Creative
* Study science or mathematics → Science
* Organize people for a project → People
* Solve an environmental problem → Environment

**Question 6: Which future sounds most exciting?**

* Discover something new → Science
* Build a company → Business
* Create products or media → Creative
* Teach or communicate with people → People

**Question 7: Which skill would you like to learn?**

* Programming or robotics → Technology
* Law or public policy → Law
* Health or psychology → Health
* Environmental technology → Environment

**Question 8: What kind of impact would you like to make?**

* Create things people enjoy → Creative
* Discover new knowledge → Science
* Solve real-world problems with technology → Technology
* Help people or communities → People

### Required features

Create these screens:

1. **Welcome screen**

   * Career Compass title
   * Short explanation
   * "Start Quiz" button

2. **Quiz screen**

   * Show one question at a time
   * Four answer choices
   * Progress indicator such as "Question 3 of 8"
   * Progress bar
   * Back button
   * Next button
   * User must select an answer before continuing

3. **Results screen**

   * Show the user's strongest career direction
   * Show a short description
   * Show a second direction to explore
   * Show the scores for all 8 directions
   * Include a "Take Quiz Again" button

### Scoring

Each selected answer gives 1 point to its assigned career direction.

Do NOT tell users that the result determines their career.

Use wording such as:

"Your strongest career direction is..."

and:

"You may enjoy..."

### Important scoring requirement

The current questions do not give every career direction exactly the same number of answer opportunities. Do not display misleading scores such as "3/8" for every category.

Either:

* normalize the scores appropriately, OR
* clearly display the relative results without pretending every category has the same maximum.

Also handle ties sensibly. If two directions have the same highest score, do not arbitrarily claim that one is definitively better.

### Design

Make the design:

* clean
* modern
* simple
* professional
* mobile-friendly
* easy for young users to understand
* not overly colorful
* good spacing
* large readable buttons
* clear progress bar

Use a simple blue-based visual style for the first version.

### Technical requirements

Use a simple web stack:

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript

Keep the code organized and beginner-friendly.

Do not require a backend for the first version.

Do not add login, payment, AI, or unnecessary features.

Make sure the complete quiz works from start to finish before adding extra features.

After creating it, explain:

1. Which files were created
2. Where the quiz questions are stored
3. Where the scoring logic is
4. How I can change a question later
5. How I can change colors/design
6. How I can publish the website and get a shareable link
